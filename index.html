Artist Story

The journey behind the music.

Who Is Teddyzhu?

Teddyzhu is a Kenyan recording artist, songwriter and creative artist born in 2007 and raised in Kisii, Kenya.

His music is influenced by love, hope, faith, memories, personal experiences and the determination to build a future through creativity.

The mission is to turn real experiences into music and connect with listeners beyond borders.

The Vision

Teddyzhu is building more than a collection of songs. The vision is a complete creative universe involving music, visuals, stories, fans, digital experiences and community.

From Kisii to Africa and eventually to audiences around the world, every release is another chapter.
