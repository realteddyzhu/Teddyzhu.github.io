TEDDYZHU

An Artist. A Story. A Journey.

Teddyzhu is the artistic name of Vincent Kaunda Ombasa, a Kenyan creative whose journey is closely connected to music and personal expression.

Born in 2007 and raised in Kenya, Teddyzhu developed a strong interest in singing and began taking music more seriously as the years went on.

His creative direction moves across different emotions and sounds, including Bongo Flava, ballads and gospel influences.

The goal is simple: create music and creative work that feels honest, personal and memorable.

BIOGRAPHY

The story behind
Teddyzhu.

Teddyzhu is a Kenyan artist born in Kisii in 2007. His artistic journey developed from a love for music into a serious pursuit of becoming a recognized independent artist.

Teddyzhu officially began his music journey in 2021. Since then, music has become an important part of his creative identity, allowing him to express emotions, experiences, ideas and stories through different sounds.

His work continues to grow around songs, visual creativity, storytelling and building a direct connection with listeners.
