About Teddyzhu

The story behind the artist

Teddyzhu is a Kenyan recording artist, songwriter and creative artist born in 2007 and raised in Kisii, Kenya.

His journey into music is connected to a life filled with challenges, dreams, emotions and experiences that shaped the person and artist he continues to become.

From an early age, music became something more than entertainment. It became a way to express feelings, tell stories and create hope. As his passion developed, Teddyzhu began working toward recording and releasing his own music.

His music explores different sides of life — including love, faith, struggle, memories, hope and ambition. Each release represents another chapter in an ongoing artistic journey.

Teddyzhu believes that difficult circumstances do not have to determine the final destination. His goal is to transform real experiences into music that can connect with listeners both in Africa and around the world.

Today, Teddyzhu continues developing his sound, visual identity and creative projects while building a global audience through digital platforms.

The Journey

From struggle to music

The Beginning

Born in 2007 and raised in Kisii, Kenya, Teddyzhu grew up experiencing circumstances that taught him resilience and the importance of holding onto dreams.

Discovering Music

Music gradually became a powerful form of expression. Singing became a way to communicate emotions and imagine a different future.

Recording & Creating

As the passion became stronger, Teddyzhu began taking recording and songwriting seriously, developing his own artistic identity through original releases.

Turning Life Into Art

Love, faith, pain, memories, struggle and hope became important themes within his creative world.

The Vision

The mission is simple: keep creating, keep improving and take Teddyzhu's music from Kisii to Africa and eventually to a global audience.
