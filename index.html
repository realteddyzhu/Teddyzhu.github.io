About Teddyzhu

The artist, the story and the vision behind the music.

Who Is Teddyzhu?

Teddyzhu is a Kenyan recording artist, songwriter and creative artist born in 2007 and raised in Kisii, Kenya.

His artistic journey is built around music, emotion, personal experiences, perseverance and the desire to reach people through sound and storytelling.

His music explores themes such as love, faith, hope, memories, struggle and ambition while continuing to develop an identity that represents his generation and his African roots.

The Vision

Teddyzhu's vision is to continue growing as an independent artist, release meaningful music and build a global audience.

The goal is not simply to release songs, but to create a complete creative world around the music — including visuals, stories, performances, digital projects and experiences for fans.

From Kisii to Africa and eventually to the world, every release represents another step in the journey.

The Journey

Every artist has a story. This is part of Teddyzhu's.

The Beginning

Born in 2007 and raised in Kisii, Kenya, Teddyzhu grew up with dreams that extended beyond his immediate circumstances.

Discovering Music

Music gradually became a powerful form of expression. Singing became a way to communicate emotions and imagine a different future.

Taking Music Seriously

The passion eventually developed into a serious creative journey, with songwriting, recording and releases becoming important parts of the process.

Turning Experiences Into Art

Love, faith, memories, loss, hope, ambition and difficult moments became sources of inspiration for the music.

The Future

Teddyzhu continues building his sound, visual identity and audience with the ambition of taking his music from Kisii to Africa and to listeners around the world.
