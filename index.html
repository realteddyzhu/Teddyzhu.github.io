ABOUT THE ARTIST

Who is Teddyzhu?

A story built from beginnings, ambition and a commitment to keep creating.

The Artist

Teddyzhu is the artist name of a Kenyan musician born in Kisii, Kenya. His musical journey officially began in 2021, marking the beginning of a serious chapter in his pursuit of music and creative expression. His identity as an artist is connected to his experiences, emotions, relationships, challenges and dreams. Through music, Teddyzhu continues to create his own path and develop a sound and identity that represents him.
